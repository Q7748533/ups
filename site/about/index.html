About ParcelRate

A shipping-rate reference built on published tariffs instead of guesses.

Where the numbers come from

Every price on this site is parsed directly from the official USPS price files published at usps.com/business/prices. Each table shows its effective date and the exact file it came from. Nothing is estimated, averaged, or carried over from an older price sheet.

What we don't do

  • We don't quote "average" shipping costs. There is no such number — weight, size and distance decide the price.
  • We don't mix price sheets from different years. If a carrier hasn't published a new tariff, we don't show it next to one that has.
  • We don't guess your commercial discount. All figures are published list rates.
  • We don't publish a table we can't parse accurately. An incomplete page is better than a wrong one.

Update cadence

Price files are re-fetched every 12 hours. Last fetch: 2026-10-22T16:28:07Z. USPS changes prices on published effective dates — we show the date from the file itself, not the date we downloaded it.

Coverage

Currently: USPS domestic services only. FedEx and UPS publish their tariffs as multi-hundred-page PDFs whose tables don't extract reliably; adding them is planned but not done. We'd rather ship one carrier correctly than three badly.
