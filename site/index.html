What makes this different

Most shipping-cost pages give you a range and a shrug. This site parses the official USPS CSV price files directly, so every table below is the real tariff — including the weight tiers, all nine zones, and the surcharges that get added on top.

Read this before you budget. These are published list rates. If you ship with a commercial account, a platform discount, or a reseller, your actual price will be lower — sometimes a lot lower. We don't guess what your discount is.
