What Ground Advantage replaced

In July 2023 USPS merged First-Class Package Service and Parcel Select Ground into Ground Advantage. Packages up to 70 lb, 2–5 business days, $100 of insurance included.

Where the price jumps

The largest per-pound jump is at the low end — 4 oz to 12 oz costs more than twice as much, because USPS prices the first tier by handling cost rather than weight.
