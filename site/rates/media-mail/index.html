What counts as Media Mail

Books with at least 8 pages, sound recordings, recorded video, printed music, and certain educational materials. Advertising is not allowed beyond incidental. USPS opens and inspects Media Mail packages — if the contents don't qualify, the package is returned or charged the higher rate.

What does not qualify

  • Blank notebooks and journals
  • Comic books with advertising
  • Board games, puzzles, and toys
  • Computer media with data or software
  • Anything with more than incidental advertising
