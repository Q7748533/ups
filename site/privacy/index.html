Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What we collect

This site is a static website. It sets no cookies of its own and has no accounts, no sign-up, and no forms that submit data anywhere. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — the weight and zone you enter never leave your device.

The zone finder works the same way. The zone table is a static file downloaded to your browser; the ZIP codes you type into it are matched on your device and are not sent to us, to USPS, or to anyone else.

Server logs

Our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. These are retained per the provider's standard policy.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in future, this section will be updated before they go live, and any analytics that require consent will not load until consent is given.

Advertising

This site currently displays no advertising. If advertising is added, this policy will be updated first, including any third-party ad vendors and their data practices.

Third-party links

Links to carrier websites and shipping services are provided for reference. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to request or delete. If that changes, this section will say how to exercise those rights.
