Cheapest way to ship furniture

Furniture is where USPS usually stops making sense — above a certain size and weight, the parcel networks price themselves out.

Effective September 14, 2026·Source: USPS official price file·Published list rate
We don't know your package. There is no such thing as "the average cost to ship furniture" — it depends on weight, box size and distance. What follows is the real published price at each weight, so you can pick your own row instead of trusting an average that was invented by someone else.

What actually drives the price

  • Dimensional weight. USPS bills the greater of actual weight and L×W×H÷166. A light but bulky box is priced as if it were heavy.
  • Zone. The same package can cost twice as much crossing the country as it does going one state over.
  • Size surcharges. Over 22 inches adds a nonstandard length fee; over 2 cubic feet adds a volume fee. Full list.

Options worth knowing

  • Ground Advantage — cheapest for most parcels, 2–5 business days.
  • Priority Mail — 1–3 days, and Flat Rate ignores weight entirely if it fits.
  • Priority Mail Express — when it has to arrive tomorrow.

All figures are USPS published list rates. Commercial or negotiated prices can be lower. Nothing here is an estimate.
